How the Raise Scoreboard works

Every week, US companies and funds file a Form D with the SEC within 15 days of first selling securities in a private (exempt) offering. This site ranks those filings like a sports league: who raised the most, which sectors and states are winning, and who is new.

What counts as a new raise

  • A new Form D notice (amendments update a company's box score but are not ranked again);
  • with money already sold (the "total amount sold" on the form);
  • whose first sale happened within about a year of the filing (older, continuing offerings are shown on box scores but not ranked);
  • that is not part of an acquisition or merger exchange (the form's business-combination box).

Raises are ranked by the amount sold as reported. Weeks run Monday to Sunday by filing date; a week is final once Friday's filings are in. Seasons are calendar years.

Leagues

Form D asks each filer to pick an industry group. Leagues group them: Tech (Other Technology, Computers, Telecommunications), Bio & Health (Biotechnology, Pharmaceuticals, Other Health Care, Hospitals and Physicians, Health Insurance), Energy, Real Estate, Finance, Main Street (retail, restaurants, manufacturing, business services, travel, agriculture), the Open League (filers who chose "Other", which includes many technology startups) and the Fund League (pooled investment funds: venture capital, private equity, hedge and other funds). States are the company's principal place of business; non-US filers are grouped as International. A rookie is a company filing its first Form D ever.

What Form D does not tell you

  • Not every raise files a Form D, and some file late; a missing company is not proof of anything.
  • "Amount sold" can include debt, convertible notes, SAFEs, options and warrants, not just priced equity, and is cumulative for the offering.
  • The filer chooses its industry group, so leagues are only as precise as those choices.
  • A Form D is a notice, not an announcement or a valuation, and it is not an offer to sell securities.

Privacy, corrections and embeds

No personal data is published: Form D lists executives and directors, and this site leaves them out. There are no cookies; page views are counted without cookies only if the operator enables it, and never when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Spotted something wrong? Open a public correction with the filing link. The weekly leaderboard and each league can be embedded on any site: the code is at the bottom of those pages.
